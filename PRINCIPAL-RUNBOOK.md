# Fine Print Mercantile — original POD production and fulfillment runbook

**Owner / effective scope (2026-10-18):** Causeway Public Consumer Resources owns the Fine Print Mercantile *instance*, its brand, product descriptions, rights, merchant choices and resulting offering acceptance. Reusable rendering and design compilation belong to [Red Cup print-design engineering](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/print-design-engineering-services-section); accepted media and Printify **draft-only** composition belong to [Causeway Public Media](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-media-services-section/blob/main/src/pod-printify.mjs). The common customer destination is [shop.561.group](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-services-section/issues/17) as an intended multi-seller human/agent shop, not a second Printify Pop-Up Store invented by a product issue. Other channels can be approved independently. This runbook is the **current operator entry** and retains [SHOP.md](SHOP.md), [LISTINGS.md](LISTINGS.md), [src/corpus.mjs](src/corpus.mjs) and original assets as authoritative source; never replace them with a copy in chat.

## Run the original source → real model → physical product process

1. **Select actual customer purpose, signal and original design.** Record maker/market observations (source URL, date, close substitute, original rights, actual user need and independent buyer evidence) under existing [Opportunity Services](https://github.com/561-group/561-group-opportunity-services-section/issues/4). Choose an existing Fine Print `src/corpus.mjs` record or an independently originated design admitted by its specific merchant/creator. `LISTINGS.md` phrases, personal fashion concepts or social likes are not consent, clearance or observed purchase.
2. **Use the qualified generator, without duplicate libraries.** Recover the producer revision and the original source/corpus/policy ownership. The Red Cup `pod-merch` source has `stats`, `id`, `niche`, `all`, `art`, `art-set`; it requires the original **explicit SEN catalogue and print policy** and an **existing instance-root** for writes, rather than embedded brand values. Its installation on the selected operating workspace must be demonstrated before treating it as the incumbent runnable producer. The historical in-tree command can be used only in its qualified older dependency closure without declaring the Red Cup extraction already adopted. A generated SVG, accepted clean raster, artwork mockup and print placement are distinct artifact classes.
3. **Admit the *physical garment blank and manufacturer* first.** Record supplier, variant size/color IDs, decoration method and available positions, actual print-area pixel dimensions/aspect, print-file DPI or vector policy, allowed color/transparency, wash/care information, return policy, sample method and updated per-size base **plus shipping, tax, processor, support and refund** costs. `SHOP.md` price tables are historical hypotheses, never live cost/retail authorization. Fail closed for a copied design, claimed sublimation that requires different material, unknown place/variant, missing rights/attribution, or art that looks correct only in an AI mockup.
   **Performed color-and-size preflight (2026-10-23; original source, not a public SKU):** All 36 original `data/state/rendered-designs/*.svg` files were directly inspected for declared text fills. In each of the six niches, five SVGs include near-black `#111111` text (30 source designs total) and one includes off-white `#f7f5f0` text (six total). Those are **screen contrast hypotheses**, not automatic garment recommendations: black text on a near-black blank and pale text on a white blank can lose legibility, and accent-text contrast can be weaker even when the headline remains visible. The first concrete preflight of source `plant-03-dirt-under-my-nails.svg` (Git blob `3dd8adc704dd759c546d49188d1634e975b0fe29`) yielded a transparent 12×15-inch image candidate SHA-256 `39d76e85451f6bdd0bd1ab0a248764c43961fe3da83b539f28905119b522b14f`; private light/dark *flat-screen* proofs exposed its black-on-dark loss of readability. **HOLD dark-shirt variant** pending actual creator approval of a different blank or correctly recolored source, verified print area, manufacturer variant and physical sample; the white-background trial is still only a hypothesis. No shirt was printed, listed or sold. [Merch Assist original consumer](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-resources-services-section/pull/1), [Red Cup print maker](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/print-design-engineering-services-section/pull/2) and [Lenticule source-result analysis](https://github.com/lenticule-science/561-theory/pull/31) retain their own custody. The Red Cup candidate producer now emits both proofs beside the transparent file; on a real operating host verify that the exact installed source and real rights-admission dependency work before relying on it.
   
   **Physical-size distinction:** public Printful support for Bella+Canvas 3001 describes 15×18-inch print area on larger sizes and 11.5×13.8-inch area on smaller sizes, with provider-side *proportional downscaling* of a larger-size design. A 300-pixel/inch local export of the large print canvas is 4500×5400 (24.3 megapixels), now within the bounded 30MP composer; this is **not** evidence that Printful is the merchant supplier or that the declared artwork will be unchanged on a smaller shirt. For every offered size/color, qualify the exact actual provider template and transform, sample/care requirements, direct current quote and seller/creator acceptance. One local PNG does not establish multiple valid physical variants.

4. **Request media and product acceptance through their actual consumers.** If art needs inference, use the existing `MediaNeed`/allowance/rights/receiver system and accepted image/asset contract; an issue or private vector file alone does not grant an image-generation or publish award. For an original accepted SKU, retain product ownership, separately verified source/media/store references, versioned variants and only then a `AcceptedPODProduct` release. Do not invent a `release.json`, supplier ID, acceptance URL, runtime secret, signer or price to unblock a provider script.
5. **Stage a provider draft only within authorized capacity.** The existing `apps/pod-printify.mjs check|stage` implements the exact typed product draft boundary for an accepted print image and Printify manufacturer/shop. External writes require a real environment, bounded idempotency/readback and separately granted authority. No `workflow_dispatch stage` or Printify source write is implied by this runbook. Important API distinction: Printify's **product `visible` field defaults to true and is documented read-only**; the source adapter currently does not confirm non-public visibility on readback. Until the provider/channel-specific visibility and release semantics are independently qualified, **do not operate a connected merchant staging write on a promise that it is a private draft**. See the manufacturer [API product fields](https://developers.printify.com/API-Doc-RREdits.html/1000) and [Public Media product source](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-media-services-section/blob/main/src/pod-printify.mjs). Opening the printed product in a provider UI is not a live shop receipt.
6. **Receive actual product and storefront independently.** Request a real sample (operator consent and any spend authority independently needed), photograph the sample, measure placement, fabric/print durability and fit across listed sizes, resolve problems and then have Group→Causeway accept final print master and authorized public listing. For `shop.561.group`, the consumer must actually serve a versioned SKU with accurate variants, rights, returns, shipping, tax/checkout and external buyer fulfillment; check a public URL and independent customer/provider events. A provider product ID is not a shop catalog or settled customer payment.
7. **Measure the commercial loop through the original Work.** Separate listing discovery, impressions, clicks, variants, conversion, actual orders, refunds, customer support, attributable payouts and net unit contribution; use the original source/consumer Work record and existing finance/commerce observers, not a new `POD-sales.json` parallel ledger. Demand is not demonstrated by a working image renderer or item count.

## Three noninterchangeable positive/alternate/refusal cases

- **Valid POD candidate:** select a Fine Print original design; resolve actual manufacturer and print method; independently obtain all rights/size/variant/placement/product acceptance; generate accepted art through real producer; stage **only** where the provider's non-public state is independently assured; eventually independently read the public product/seller/fulfillment result.
- **Alternate medium:** [Transit Layer cut-and-sew apparel model](../content/designs/apparel/transit-layer-v0-1/CONSTRUCTION.md) is native sewn garment pattern CAD + fabric + fit + tested assembly; it **cannot** be sent to the Printify artwork uploader as if vector art were clothing construction. [CITY LOOPS](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/issues/117) has separate knitted row instructions and real knit-fit gates.
- **Refuse:** no source rights, no physical variant/proven print area, loss-making under known accepted terms, guessed external IDs, an unapproved issue, wrong shop account, no verified non-public provider draft, or a historical price quote must stop before external product/order writes. Missing data is UNKNOWN, not “we'll fill it in later”.
